About All Green Foundation

Our mission

All Green Foundation is a community-driven initiative dedicated to sustainable gardening, food security, and environmental education.

Our garden provides a practical place for people to develop gardening skills, work together, and learn to protect natural resources.

Explore our garden, our activities, or contact us.

Our goals

  • Encourage sustainable gardening practices.
  • Share gardening and environmental knowledge.
  • Support community participation and food growing.
  • Promote responsible use of soil and water.
